Executive Summary
The program has achieved 42.3% migration completion (1,203 of 2,847 tenants) as of the June 15 reporting date, with the Enterprise tier leading at 78% and Dev/Test significantly lagging at 18%. 341 tenants are classified as At Risk, carrying active Synapse workloads with fewer than 30 days to the read-only phase transition. The program is currently in Phase 3 (Active Migration Window); coordinated outreach is underway with focus on centralindia and brazilsouth regions, which remain most critically behind schedule.
